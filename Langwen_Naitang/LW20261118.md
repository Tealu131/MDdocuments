####  双宾语：show  sb.  sth. == show  sth.  to  sb.  
- show  you a book   ==  show  a  book  to you
- give me an  apple  ==  give an apple  to me
- pass me  a  ruler  ==  pass a  ruler  to me 
- take him  an  eraser  == take  an  eraser  to him
#### 祈使句：
- Open the door.  
- Sit down please.  == Please  sit down.  
- Don't run.  &nbsp;&nbsp; Don't  move.  &nbsp;&nbsp;  Don't  worry.
